Aland Home Finder

Houses for sale in Mariehamn and Jomala. Asking prices from Åland brokers featuring energy declarations and heating systems. Always confirm details on the broker's page.

Notes. Prices are asking prices (utgångspris). Buyer also pays transfer tax (3% for houses, 1.5% for housing-company shares) and registration fees. Some Åland properties require "hembygdsrätt" (right of domicile) for non-residents; ask the broker.

Row and semi-detached homes are shares in a housing company, with a monthly charge and sometimes a leased plot, so they are legally different from a detached house on its own plot.

Energy declarations and heating systems are sourced from official broker listings and energy inspections.
